# Audio credits

## rain-loop.m4a

Rain with thunder, from **"Rain thunder and other noises"**.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_thunder_and_other_noises.ogg
- License: **Public domain**. Attribution not required; kept as provenance.

Processing: high-passed at 30 Hz, normalised to -24 LUFS (background level,
peaks capped at -3 dBTP so the thunder has room), then closed into a seamless
176s loop by cross-fading the last 4s over the first 4s.

Kept at full length deliberately — the thunder rolls about every 18s, so a
short loop would make the same crack repeat obviously.
