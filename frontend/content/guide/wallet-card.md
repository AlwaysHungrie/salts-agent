You can buy credits for your agents straight from your Tokens Wallet. Payments go through Razorpay. In India they are charged in rupees, and you can pay by card, UPI or netbanking. Everywhere else they are charged in US dollars and you pay with PayPal. Salts works out which one to show from where you are connecting from, so it never asks for your location. Your credits arrive as a new key, loaded with what you paid for, as soon as your payment goes through.

## 1. Open the checkout

Open **Tokens Wallet** from the top right corner of the home page. Under **Get new API key**, click **Pay with UPI or card** in India, or **Pay with Card** anywhere else.

## 2. Choose how much credit you want

Under **Credit**, type an amount or click one of the suggested amounts. In India the amount is in rupees and we recommend starting with ₹225. Elsewhere it is in US dollars, from $5 to $100, and we recommend starting with $25. You can always buy another key later once you have a feel for how much your agent uses.

Below the amount you can see the credit that will be added to your key, the service fee and the total you pay. In rupees, the total in US dollars is shown under **In USD** too. Your key holds its credit in US dollars, so it shows the dollar amount once it is in your wallet. Credits once issued cannot be transferred or refunded.

## 3. Pay

Click the **Pay** button, which shows your total, such as **Pay ₹243.00** or **Pay $27.00**. Razorpay's payment window opens. In India, choose a card, UPI or netbanking. Elsewhere, pay with PayPal. Finish the payment there. The button changes to **Confirming payment…** while Salts checks the payment with Razorpay, which usually takes a few seconds.

## You are done

When you see **Your key has been added to your wallet.**, the new key is ready. Go back to your wallet to see it, then pick it by its nickname when you [create your agent](/guide/step-by-step-agent) or from your agent's **Settings**.

If you closed the page before the key arrived, your payment is not lost. Salts hears about it from Razorpay and puts the key in your wallet within a few minutes.

## Footnotes

- Salts creates a bought key for you and never shows you the key itself, so it can only be used from your Salts wallet.
- A bought key cannot be topped up. When its credit runs out, buy a new key and switch your agent to it.
- Purchases cannot be refunded, and credit on a bought key cannot be moved to another key.
