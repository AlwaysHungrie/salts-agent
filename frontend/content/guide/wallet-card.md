If you would rather not create an OpenRouter account of your own, or pay with crypto, you can buy a key straight from your Salts wallet with a card, UPI or netbanking. Payments go through Razorpay and are charged in Indian rupees. The key comes loaded with the credit you paid for, and it appears in your wallet as soon as your payment goes through.

## 1. Open the checkout

Open your **OpenRouter wallet** from the top right corner of the home page. Under **Get new key**, click **Pay with card or UPI**.

## 2. Choose how much credit you want

Under **Credit**, type an amount in US dollars or click one of the suggested amounts. We recommend starting with $10, since you can always buy another key later once you have a feel for how much your agent uses.

Below the amount you can see the credit that will be added to your key, the service fee, the total in dollars, and what you pay **In rupees**. The service fee covers routing and payment costs.

## 3. Pay

Click the **Pay** button, which shows the total in rupees, such as **Pay ₹972.00**. Razorpay's payment window opens. Choose a card, UPI or netbanking and finish the payment there. The button changes to **Confirming payment…** while Salts checks the payment with Razorpay, which usually takes a few seconds.

## You are done

When you see **Payment received. Your key is in your wallet.**, the new key is ready. Go back to your wallet to see it, then pick it by its nickname when you [create your agent](/guide/step-by-step-agent) or from your agent's **Settings**.

If you closed the page before the key arrived, your payment is not lost. Salts hears about it from Razorpay and puts the key in your wallet within a few minutes.

## Footnotes

- Salts creates a bought key for you and never shows you the key itself, so it can only be used from your Salts wallet.
- A bought key cannot be topped up. When its credit runs out, buy a new key and switch your agent to it.
- Credit on a bought key cannot be refunded or moved to another key.
