Your users do not see a fleet. They see an agent that is theirs, with a few settings set by someone else. This page describes their side, so you can explain it to them and answer their questions.

## Getting in

A user signs in to Salts with the email address you entered for them. Their agent is on their home page, ready to use. They do not need an OpenRouter account, a card or any setup, as long as the agent uses your key.

They can talk to it in the browser straight away. To talk to it on their phone, they connect their own Telegram bot or WhatsApp number from their agent's **Settings**, the same way you would.

## What tells them it is a fleet agent

- At the top of their agent's sidebar, a line reads **This agent is part of** followed by your fleet's name.
- In **Settings**, they see their monthly spending: how much of their limit is left, marked **Set by agent admin**.
- Under **Manage access**, they see how many more people they can add, if you set a member limit.

They do not see your email address. They only see the fleet's name.

## What they can do

Everything you did not lock. They can rename their agent, pick a model from the list you allowed, write their own custom instructions, switch capabilities on and off, connect apps, and add people under **Manage access**, up to your member limit.

Anything you locked is simply not there. There is no greyed-out setting, and no message saying it is locked.

## What they cannot do

- Change a setting you locked.
- Raise their spending limit or their member limit.
- Delete their agent. Only you can.
- See any other agent in the fleet.

## What they should know about you

Be clear with your users about two things.

**You can change their settings at any time.** When you save the fleet's settings, every agent is overwritten, including the settings the user chose for themselves. A model they picked, instructions they wrote or a capability they switched on can change without warning. If you change something, tell them.

**You cannot read their conversations.** You can see that their agent exists and what it spends. You cannot open it, and you cannot see what they say to it or what it says back. Their memory and their connected apps are theirs too.

## When they run out

When an agent reaches its monthly spend limit, it replies that it has reached its limit and stops answering. It starts again at the beginning of the next calendar month, or as soon as you raise the limit for that agent.

## Footnotes

1. If a user adds someone under **Manage access**, that person can read everything in the agent, including the user's own conversations. Make sure your users know this.
2. You can send your users to this guide. [Create your first agent](/guide/getting-started) and the Telegram and WhatsApp pages apply to them too, except that their agent already exists.
