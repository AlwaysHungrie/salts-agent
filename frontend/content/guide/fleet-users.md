Your users do not see a fleet. They see an agent of their own, with a few settings chosen by you. This page describes what they see, so you can explain it to them and answer their questions.

## Getting started

Your users sign in to Salts with the email address you entered for them, and their agent is already on their home page. If the agent uses your OpenRouter key, they do not need to set anything up and can start chatting straight away.

To talk to their agent from their phone, they can connect their own Telegram bot or WhatsApp number from their agent's **Settings**. You can send them [Connect your agent to Telegram](/guide/telegram) or [Connect your agent to WhatsApp](/guide/whatsapp) to help them.

## How they know it is a fleet agent

- At the top of their agent's sidebar, they see **This agent is part of** followed by your fleet's name. They do not see your email address.
- In **Settings**, they see how much of their monthly spending limit is left, marked **Set by agent admin**.
- Under **Manage access**, they see how many more people they can add, if you set a member limit.

## What they can and cannot change

Your users can change any setting you did not lock. For example, they can rename their agent, pick a model from the list you allowed, write their own custom instructions, switch capabilities on and off, connect apps and add people under **Manage access**.

Settings you locked are hidden from them completely. They also cannot raise their spending limit or member limit, delete their agent, or see any other agent in the fleet.

## What to tell your users

We recommend being clear with your users about two things:

- **You can change their settings.** When you save the fleet's settings, every agent is overwritten, including settings the user chose themselves. Let them know before you change anything.
- **You cannot read their conversations.** You can see that their agent exists and how much it spends, but you cannot open it or see what they say to it.

It is also worth telling them that anyone they add under **Manage access** will be able to read all of their conversations.

## When they reach their limit

When an agent reaches its monthly spend limit, it tells the user and stops replying. It starts working again at the beginning of the next calendar month, or as soon as you raise the limit for that agent.
