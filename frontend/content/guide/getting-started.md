This guide will help you create your first agent and talk to it in your browser. It takes about five minutes. You will need an OpenRouter key. [Get an OpenRouter key](/guide/openrouter) explains how to get one. No technical skills are required.

> What to expect:
>
> - Your agent runs in the cloud. It keeps working when your laptop is closed.
> - Every agent is separate. It has its own chats, its own memory, its own settings and its own keys. Nothing is shared between two agents.
> - Every reply your agent writes is billed to your OpenRouter key. Salts does not charge you for the model.

## 1. Sign in

Open the Salts home page and click **Connect your Account**. Sign in with the account you want your agents to belong to.

## 2. Create the agent

Click **Create a new Agent**.

1. Enter a name for your agent. You can change it later.
2. Leave **Create a fleet of agents** unchecked. Fleets are for running agents for other people, and are explained in [What is a fleet](/guide/fleets).
3. Click **Next**.
4. Paste your OpenRouter key. If you do not have one yet, follow [Get an OpenRouter key](/guide/openrouter) first. You can also leave it empty and add it later in settings.
5. Click **Create**.

Your agent now shows up in the list on the home page. Click it to open it.

## 3. Send your first message

Click **Start a chat** and ask your agent anything. The reply shows up in the same window, along with what it cost.

Every chat is a session. Sessions are listed in the sidebar on the left, and each one keeps its own history. Start a new session whenever you change subject. See [Commands](/guide/commands) for why that matters.

## 4. Find your way around

Your agent has two pages besides the chat. Both are in the sidebar.

| Page | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | Connect Telegram or WhatsApp, pick the model, write custom instructions, change the OpenRouter key, and choose who else can use the agent. |
| **Capabilities** | Switch on what your agent can do besides write: web search, reading links and files, images, voice notes, scheduled tasks, memory and MCP servers. |

Most capabilities are off when you create an agent. Switch on only what you need. Every capability you switch on adds instructions the model has to read on every turn, which makes each reply a little more expensive.

## You are done

From here, most people do one of two things:

- Talk to the agent from their phone. Follow [Connect your agent to Telegram](/guide/telegram) or [Connect your agent to WhatsApp](/guide/whatsapp).
- Give the agent the live web. Follow [Set up web search](/guide/web-search).

## Footnotes

1. A free account can only create a limited number of agents. If you need more, click **Need more agents?** on the home page and request a business account.
2. **Manage access** in settings lets you add other people by email. Anyone on that list can chat with the agent, change its settings and read every message in it, including yours. Only add people you trust with all of that.
