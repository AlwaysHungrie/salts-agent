So far, this guide has been about one agent: yours. A fleet is how you give agents to other people.

Say you run a team of ten. You want everyone on the team to have their own AI agent. You do not want ten people each signing up for OpenRouter, adding a card, and working out which settings to pick. With a fleet, you create all ten agents in one go, one for each person. You pay for them, and you decide how they are set up.

## Two words used on these pages

- **You** are the **fleet admin**. You create the fleet, pay for it and manage it.
- **Users** are the people you create agents for. Each user gets one agent.

## What each user gets

Each user gets a complete agent of their own, exactly like the one you made for yourself in [Create your first agent](/guide/getting-started). They sign in to Salts with their email address, and their agent is waiting for them.

Their agent is private to them. It has its own conversations, its own memory, and its own Telegram bot or WhatsApp number if they connect one. No user can see another user's agent.

**You cannot see their conversations either.** As the fleet admin, you control how the agents are set up and what they can spend. You cannot open a user's agent or read what they say to it.

## What you control

- **Who pays.** Every agent in the fleet can use your OpenRouter key, so users do not need an account of their own.
- **How much each agent can spend** in a month.
- **Which models** users can choose from.
- **Which settings users can change.** You can lock any setting. A locked setting is hidden from the user and stays the way you set it.
- **Who gets an agent.** You can add people at any time, and remove their agent.

You can change all of this at any time, for every agent at once or for one agent at a time.

## When a fleet is the right choice

- You want to give AI agents to your team, and keep the cost under control.
- You want to give an agent to each of your customers, set up for your product.
- You are setting agents up for friends or family who would never create an OpenRouter account themselves.

If you only want to share your own agent with one or two people, you do not need a fleet. Add them under **Manage access** in your agent's settings. They will be able to read everything in it, so only do this with people you trust.
