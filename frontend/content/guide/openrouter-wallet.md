Every reply your agent writes is paid for from credit. You buy that credit under **AI credits**, and it arrives as a key: a balance with a nickname that you can give to any of your agents.

For example, you might keep one key for your personal agent and another for an agent you share with your team, each with its own credit, and see at a glance how much each one has left.

## Why you need a key in your wallet

An agent without a key cannot reply to anything. When you create an agent, the **Key** dropdown lists the keys in your wallet, so you need at least one there first. If your wallet is empty, the dropdown says **No keys in your wallet**, and you can open the wallet straight from it to add one.

The first time you create an agent, we give you a small key called **Starter key**. If it is the only key in your wallet, it is already picked in the dropdown, so your first agent can reply straight away. It holds a few cents of credit, enough to try a low-cost model such as DeepSeek or GLM Flash, so add a key of your own once you know you want to keep going. Everyone gets the starter key once, and removing it doesn't bring it back.

## Opening your wallet

On the Salts home page, click **AI credits** in the top right corner. You can also open it from the wallet icon at the end of any **Key** dropdown.

Each key in your wallet has a nickname, such as "Amber Falcon", so you can tell your keys apart. Under the nickname you can see how much credit the key has left, and how many of your agents use it.

## Getting credits

There are three ways to add credits, all under **Get new key**:

- **Pay with UPI or card.** Pick how much credit you want in rupees and pay by card, UPI or netbanking through Razorpay. See [Buy credits with UPI or card](/guide/wallet-card).
- **Pay with crypto.** Pick how much credit you want and pay with USDC or ETH from your crypto wallet. See [Buy credits with crypto](/guide/wallet-crypto).
- **Bring your own OpenRouter key.** If you already pay for models on OpenRouter, choose **Connect OpenRouter account**, or paste a key under **Already have an OpenRouter key?**. See [Bring your own OpenRouter key](/guide/openrouter).

When you buy credits, Salts creates the key for you and puts it straight into your wallet.

## Removing a key

Click **Remove** on a key to take it out of your wallet. Every agent that uses the key stops replying until you give it another one, so we recommend picking a new key for those agents first.

A key you bought lives only in your wallet, so removing it also deletes it, and any credit left on it is lost. Salts asks you to type the key's nickname before it does this.
